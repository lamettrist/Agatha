# Data licences (external and team data)

Scope: every dataset that was considered for Part C (external physics data for evaluating the learned
world model), whether it ended up being used or not. All licence statements below were read at the
listed URL on **2026-11-15** (and re-read by a second reviewer the same day). "Verified" means the licence text was read at an official source of the
dataset or of its repository; caveats are written out where the source does not state a dedicated
dataset licence.

**No arbitrary scraped internet video is used anywhere in this project.** Only the datasets listed
here (downloaded from their official hosts) and clips recorded by the team itself are allowed. Nothing
from the external datasets is committed to the repository: raw downloads live under `data/external/`
and converted shards under `data/external_v2/` (both git-ignored). The shipped box model (`models/contact_gnn.pt`) has not been trained or
fine-tuned on any of this data; for it the data is used for approximate evaluation only
(`docs/part-c-external-data.md`). Exception: the EXPERIMENTAL soft-body checkpoint `models/soft_gnn.pt` WAS trained with the converted MeshGraphNets `sphere_simple` cloth data mixed in (5% of each batch, 80 trajectories), by team decision, although that data has no explicit licence (see its row and `docs/soft-bodies-experimental.md`); that checkpoint is not publicly released. Exception added later the same day: the rows marked **VIDEO-DERIVED** below were used to train separate EXPERIMENTAL fine-tune arms (`docs/video-training.md`), none of which was kept; the shipped box model is not trained on them.

## Notes

- **Video.** The only videos used are (a) the RGB renders that belong to the Kubric MOVi-A and CLEVRER datasets above, downloaded from their official hosts, and (b) the team's own clip. No video site was scraped. An additional Exa search for openly licensed REAL videos of boxes falling / sliding was NOT carried out in the video-pipeline time budget, so no real third-party video is used.

- Downloads are treated as untrusted input: they are parsed with numpy (`allow_pickle=False`), `json`,
  `h5py`, `torch.load(weights_only=True)` (ContactNets tensors) and, for RBO, `csv` / `tarfile` member
  reads / `xml.etree` only; nothing inside them is executed.
- **Restriction summary.** Non-commercial: I.AM. (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Repository-level licence only, no
  dedicated data licence: Kubric, Physion, ContactNets. Unclear or absent: MeshGraphNets data, GGNS, MBDS,
  Physion++. Every dataset in the first two groups is limited to evaluation (plus simulator-parameter
  fitting for the two real box sets); the third group is not used at all, with ONE exception: the
  MeshGraphNets `sphere_simple` cloth data is used for training / evaluating the experimental, non-released
  soft-body model by team decision (2026-11-15) under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence - its data licence
  itself remains unstated (see its row). Explicit and permissive: CLEVRER
  (CC0), RBO (CC BY 4.0) - also evaluation only here, for technical reasons.
- The "repository-level" licences (Kubric: Apache-2.0, Physion: MIT, ContactNets: BSD-3-Clause) are recorded with their caveat
  on purpose: we found no separate licence statement for the rendered / simulated data of either project,
  so whether the repository licence covers the hosted data files is our reading, not an explicit grant. If a
  stricter reading is required, CLEVRER (explicit CC0) and I.AM. (explicit CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) are the ones
  with an unambiguous dataset licence.
- Apache-2.0 and MIT require the licence / copyright notice to accompany redistributed copies. We do not
  redistribute any of the data (raw or converted); if converted shards are ever shared, include the
  upstream licence texts and the citations above.
- Kubric, CLEVRER and Physion are **simulator output** (PyBullet, Blender/Bullet, TDW/PhysX). The I.AM.
  and ContactNets rows are **real-world pose tracks** (motion capture / AprilTags) of a single tossed box;
  they contain no video. Results derived from any of them are labelled approximate everywhere.
- The I.AM. and ContactNets datasets were discovered with Exa (`scripts/find_physics_datasets.py`, raw
  results in `data/external/exa_dataset_search.json`); their licences were then read on the official pages
  listed above (not in search snippets) and re-read by a second pass on the same day.
- I.AM. is **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: non-commercial use only and share-alike. It is the most restrictive licence
  in this table. It is compatible with the evaluation done here; it would have to be re-assessed before any
  commercial use of a model that was trained on it (no model has been).
